FunderPro Affiliate Program FAQs

The answers to the most common questions about our affiliate partnership program

More Affiliate FAQs
What Are the Payout Methods Available?

All payouts are issued in UDSC (ERC-20 standard).

What Is the Minimum Earning Required for a Payout?

The minimum amount required to receive a payout is $50.

How Often Can I Claim a Payout for My Commissions?

Commissions are paid automatically into your USDC wallet every Thursday. You don’t need to request a withdrawal.

Do You Provide Logos and Materials That I Can Use?

Yes! Contact your Affiliate Manager, he will provide you with a full media kit.

Are Paid Promotion and Advertisement Allowed?

We encourage you to foster organic reach, as that yields the best long-term results. Nonetheless, you can use paid ads to promote your affiliate link. However, there are restrictions outlined in section 6 of the Affiliate Terms and Conditions.

How Can I Track My Performance?

Our state-of-the-art affiliate dashboard will provide you with accurate information on how your links and coupons are performing. You can even attach custom UTMs and Tracking Codes to your link to monitor different platforms.

Who Can Join Your Affiliate Program?
The FunderPro Affiliate Program can be joined by anyone 18 years old or older. However, we cannot offer you this service if you reside in one of the countries listed at this link.
I Have a Large Community and Network. Can I Get a Custom Deal?

Our Affiliate Program has an in-built scaling system that rewards you with higher commission as you scale up your earnings. If your performance is outstanding, we may be able to offer you a custom deal, but we will always ask to prove yourself first.

What Is Last-Click Attribution?

Last-Click attribution means that the sale is attributed to the owner of the coupon used at each purchase. If no coupon is used, the sale is attributed to the owner of the link used.
